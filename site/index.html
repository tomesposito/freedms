AI

Less typing. Less clicking. Less grind.

The point of AI in freeDMS is not AI features. It is less work for the dealer.

  • Quick entry, live today. Type “stock a 2019 Silverado, cost $18,500, list $24,995” or snap a photo of a window sticker — you get a filled draft. Nothing saves until you confirm it.
  • Drafted follow-ups. Stale-lead texts and emails, written for you, sent only when a person approves them.
  • Coming: conversational commands and a morning brief — what is rotting on the lot, and who is waiting on you.
  • Metered honestly. AI is the one thing that costs money: pay-as-you-go, with hard spending limits you set. The core DMS is free whether you use AI or not.

How it’s free

Free is the business model, not the trial

A free product this complete has to make money somewhere. Here is exactly where — no fine print.

The exchange

Anonymized data, aggregated

On the free tier, your operational data contributes to anonymized, aggregated benchmarks — never identifiable, never your customers, never a competitor’s prices aimed at you. Benchmarks open only when enough dealers contribute to protect everyone in the pool.

Your choice

Pay to restrict sharing

Prefer your data stays entirely yours? A paid tier restricts sharing. Your dealership keeps the full, free DMS either way — restriction changes contribution, not features.

The line

What we never do

We never sell identifiable dealer or consumer data. No lead lists. No raw transaction feeds. No per-query tolls on your own records, and full export is free forever — leaving is as easy as staying.

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers

Is it really free? What’s the catch?

The DMS is free — every module, unlimited users. The business makes money two ways: anonymized, aggregated data benchmarks on the free tier (with a paid option to restrict sharing), and pay-as-you-go AI features. If neither ever earns a cent from you, you still keep the whole system.

Do you sell my dealership’s data?

Never identifiable data — not yours, not your customers’. Free-tier data contributes only to anonymized aggregates released under minimum-cohort rules, so no cell ever describes a single dealer. And a paid tier lets you restrict sharing entirely.

Is it really open source?

Yes — MIT licensed, developed in public on GitHub. Read the code, open an issue, fork it, or run your own copy. The hosted service at freeDMS.co runs the same code.

Can I get my data out?

Any time, in full, for free: structured exports, documents, accounting files, plus an open API and webhooks. A DMS that locks your data in is a landlord, not a tool.

I’m an independent dealer. Is this built for me?

You’re the point. freeDMS starts with independent and upstart dealers — full accounting, service, and parts included, without the franchise price tag.

What about my lenders, listings, and credit pulls?

The feeds and records are built in: listing-feed export, credit-application workflow, an open connector framework. Live connections to specific networks (lender rails, bureaus, marketplaces) roll out partner by partner — and freeDMS never charges an integration fee on top.
